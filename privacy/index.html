Privacy

How this website works and how to contact Innovizion about your information.

Who to contact

Innovizion Inc., Kingston, Ontario, Canada. For privacy questions or requests about information provided to us, email vibhuanand@outlook.com with “Privacy enquiry” in the subject. Do not include sensitive information in your initial request.

The enquiry builder

The contact form creates an email draft locally in your browser. It does not send the form to a server, save it in browser storage or subscribe you to a mailing list. Choosing “Open email app” passes the draft to your configured email application. Choosing “Copy email text” copies it to your device’s clipboard. You decide whether to send it.

Information you choose to send

A business enquiry may include your name, organization, work email, optional telephone number and a short description of the requested services. These details are used to respond to the enquiry and discuss an engagement. Please provide only information relevant to that purpose.

Hosting, email and technical data

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and request information to deliver and secure the website. Email is handled by the sender’s and recipient’s email providers under their respective terms and privacy policies. These services may process information outside Canada. This website does not promise Canadian-only storage or processing.

We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to this website. Links to external services are subject to those services’ privacy practices.

Retention and your requests

Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you have provided, or to withdraw consent to further contact. Any applicable retention obligations and limits on a request will need to be considered. Your enquiry is not an instruction to send marketing communications.

Sensitive information

Please do not submit Protected, classified, export-controlled or other sensitive information through the form or ordinary email. If a potential engagement needs special handling, request an appropriate communication process first.

Questions and concerns

Send questions about these practices to vibhuanand@outlook.com. You can also consult the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada for information about Canadian privacy rights.
