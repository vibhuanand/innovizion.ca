About Innovizion

A specialist firm.
A hands-on way of working.

Canadian-owned. Based in Kingston, Ontario. Built around senior engineering expertise and practical delivery.

Who we are

Close to the work.
Accountable for the detail.

Innovizion is a technology consultancy focused on the infrastructure, security and operating practices behind dependable cloud platforms.

Our work connects architecture to implementation: networking, identity, Terraform, Kubernetes, delivery pipelines, observability and recovery. We help teams understand the dependencies and make changes they can maintain.

Personnel delivery experience spans government, financial services, digital identity, aviation and large enterprise environments. Engagements can range from a focused technical assessment to project delivery or an embedded senior specialist.

As a small specialist firm, we provide direct access to senior technical expertise and keep the working relationship close to the engineering.

How we think

Practical principles.
Consistent engineering.

Secure.

Build controls into delivery

Identity, networking, secrets, access and policy are part of platform engineering. Evidence should follow the implementation.

Repeatable.

Make change reviewable

Use infrastructure as code, clear environments and controlled releases. Document decisions so the next engineer can work confidently.

Recoverable.

Plan beyond deployment

Understand failure modes, monitor meaningful signals and make recovery procedures something the team can exercise.
